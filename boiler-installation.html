The right replacement for your property

An older, inefficient or repeatedly failing boiler may be a candidate for replacement. Jack assesses the installation and discusses suitable options before quoting for the work required.

From removal to commissioning

Work can include removal of the old boiler, a new appliance and appropriate flue, pipework alterations, system flushing and treatment, antifreeze or inhibitor protection, commissioning and relevant safety checks.

Handover and documentation

The completed installation is tested and commissioned in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions, with relevant documentation supplied. Manufacturer warranties apply to new appliances where applicable; the terms depend on the appliance.

Do you work on static caravans and holiday homes?

Yes. Static caravans, holiday homes and lodges are a main specialism. Jack also works on domestic LPG properties and with caravan and holiday parks.

Do you cover the whole of Anglesey?

Yes, including Holyhead, Valley, Trearddur Bay, Rhosneigr, Llangefni, Amlwch, Benllech, Menai Bridge and Beaumaris. Selected work in Bangor and wider North Wales is available by arrangement. Any additional travel costs are agreed beforehand.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Your name, contact number, location, property type and a brief description of the work. For caravan sites, include the park and plot number. Appliance make, model and photographs are useful when available.
