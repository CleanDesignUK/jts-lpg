YOUR LPG QUESTIONS.
ANSWERED SIMPLY.

Helpful answers about services, prices, coverage and what to send when booking. For advice on your own installation, contact Jack.

Do you work on static caravans and holiday homes?

Yes. Static caravans, holiday homes and lodges are a main specialism. Jack also works on domestic LPG properties and with caravan and holiday parks.

How much is an LPG gas safety certificate?

An LPG gas safety inspection and certificate costs £85. A certificate is provided on satisfactory completion. Combine it with the annual service of one appliance for £130, saving £40 compared with booking separately.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. JTS LPG Services is Gas Safe registered, number 958399. You can check the business and the engineer’s current LPG categories on the Gas Safe Register.

Do you cover the whole of Anglesey?

Yes, including Holyhead, Valley, Trearddur Bay, Rhosneigr, Llangefni, Amlwch, Benllech, Menai Bridge and Beaumaris. Selected work in Bangor and wider North Wales is available by arrangement. Any additional travel costs are agreed beforehand.

How often should my caravan appliances be serviced?

Generally annually, while following the appliance manufacturer’s specific servicing requirements. A service and a gas safety inspection serve different purposes; rented holiday accommodation may also have specific safety and certification requirements.

Can I book several caravans at the same park?

Yes. Jack can organise inspections, servicing and other LPG work across multiple caravans or lodges. Discounted rates are available for multiple units completed at the same location.

What is included in a breakdown call-out?

From £85 includes an Anglesey call-out and up to the first hour on site for diagnosis or repair. If the repair fits within that hour and needs no parts, there is no further labour charge. Parts and further work are discussed before proceeding.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Your name, contact number, location, property type and a brief description of the work. For caravan sites, include the park and plot number. Appliance make, model and photographs are useful when available.

Do you offer plumbing services?

Jack can provide associated plumbing and waste connections for newly sited static caravans, holiday homes and lodges, alongside LPG connection and commissioning. General domestic plumbing and small plumbing repairs are not offered as standalone services.

How do I receive my certificate?

Certificates and invoices are provided digitally. Jack’s certification system also provides reminders when the next gas safety inspection is due.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

Contact Jack to check availability. Normal hours are Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm, with Saturday by appointment. Sunday is closed, with emergency enquiries considered where possible. There is no guaranteed 24-hour emergency service.
