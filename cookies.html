COOKIES & BROWSER STORAGE.

This site uses essential browser storage to remember privacy choices and help prevent repeated form submissions. No advertising or analytics tracking is enabled in this version.

Your privacy choice

The local storage entry jts-cookie-consent-v1 records your preference and the time of your choice. The preference expires after six months. You can reopen Cookie settings in the footer at any time and change or withdraw optional consent.

Enquiry protection

The session storage entry jts-last-enquiry records the time of a successful form submission to discourage rapid duplicate submissions. It expires with your browser session. It does not contain your form answers. When the form is enabled, hCaptcha helps protect against bots and may use security-related cookies and similar technologies.

Optional analytics

Analytics is disabled at present. The consent controls can support analytics if it is configured in the future, but its script only loads after an affirmative analytics choice. The notice and privacy information should describe any analytics service before it is activated.

External services

Image placeholders load from Pexels. Social, Google and WhatsApp links open external services governed by their own privacy and cookie policies. No social feeds or map embeds load automatically.
