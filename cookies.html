This Cookie Policy explains how the JTS LPG Services website uses cookies, browser storage and similar technologies and how you can control your choices.

Last updated 14 September 2026
01 What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites may store on your computer, smartphone or other device. They can be used to remember choices, provide website functionality and, where enabled, understand how a website is being used.

Similar technologies, including local browser storage, may perform comparable functions and are covered by this policy where appropriate.

02 How does this website use cookies or browser storage?

The JTS LPG Services website may use essential browser storage to remember settings such as the privacy or cookie choice you make through the website's cookie notice.

Essential storage is used only where required to provide website functionality or remember a choice you have requested.

03 Essential cookies and storage

Essential cookies or browser storage support functions that are necessary for the website to work properly or to remember a privacy choice made by you.

These technologies do not require consent where they are strictly necessary to provide a service or functionality you have requested.

Where possible, JTS LPG Services limits essential storage to what is reasonably required for the website.

04 Analytics cookies

Optional analytics technologies may be used in the future to help understand how people use the website, such as which pages are visited and how visitors find the site.

Optional analytics should not be activated until you have given the required consent.

At the time this policy was last updated, the website code provided for JTS LPG Services does not intentionally activate optional analytics through the page before a visitor has made a consent choice.

05 Advertising and tracking cookies

JTS LPG Services does not need advertising or behavioural tracking cookies to provide the core functionality of this website.

If advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced in future, this policy and the website's consent controls should be updated before they are enabled.

06 Third-party websites

The website contains links to external services including Google, Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

Simply linking to another website does not mean that JTS LPG Services controls the cookies used on that external service.

When you leave this website and visit another provider, that provider may use its own cookies or tracking technologies in accordance with its own privacy and cookie policies.

07 Website forms and Web3Forms

Website contact forms may use Web3Forms to transmit an enquiry to JTS LPG Services.

Form processing is separate from optional advertising or analytics cookies. Personal information submitted through forms is covered by the JTS LPG Services Privacy Policy .

08 Choosing “Essential only”

If you select Essential only on the website's cookie notice, optional technologies should remain disabled and only storage required for core functionality or remembering your choice should be used.

09 Choosing “Allow optional”

If optional analytics or other non-essential technologies are introduced, selecting Allow optional may permit those technologies to operate in accordance with the information provided in this policy and the cookie notice.

Consent should be obtained before non-essential cookies or similar technologies are placed or accessed.

10 How can I manage cookies?

You can use the website's cookie notice to choose whether optional technologies are permitted where they are available.

You can also remove cookies or stored website data through your browser settings. The exact process depends on the browser and device you use.

Blocking all browser storage may affect some website functions or mean that your privacy preference cannot be remembered between visits.

11 Changes to the cookies we use

The technologies used by this website may change as the site develops.

If new analytics, advertising or other non-essential technologies are introduced, JTS LPG Services should review the cookie notice and this policy and obtain any consent required before those technologies are used.

12 Contact JTS LPG Services

If you have a question about cookies, privacy or personal information used through this website, contact JTS LPG Services at jack@jtslpg.co.uk or telephone 07498 579460.

You can also read our Privacy Policy for further information about how personal information is handled.
