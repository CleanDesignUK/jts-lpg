I started JTS LPG Services to offer holiday-home owners and parks a reliable, straightforward and professional LPG service. As an independent business, I’m the person you speak to and the person carrying out the work.

With nine years working with LPG, I cover annual safety inspections and servicing through to fault finding, repairs, boiler replacements and new caravan connections.

I take pride in being friendly and approachable, explaining the work clearly and making the process straightforward. Much of my work comes through recommendations and repeat customers.
