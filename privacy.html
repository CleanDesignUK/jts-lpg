PRIVACY NOTICE

JTS LPG Services · Last updated 10 September 2026

YOUR ENQUIRY & YOUR INFORMATION.

JTS LPG Services, run by Jack Sabine on Anglesey, is responsible for the personal information supplied when you contact the business. For privacy enquiries, email jack@jtslpg.co.uk or call 07498 579460.

Information you provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, phone number, email address, location and the service you need. You may also provide property details, a park or plot number and a message. Photos can be sent separately by WhatsApp. Please only send information relevant to the job.

Why the information is used

Your information is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, arrange work and communicate about the service. Processing necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, or to provide an agreed service, is based on that contractual purpose. Necessary business administration and proportionate fraud or spam prevention may rely on legitimate interests. Records required by applicable law are retained on that legal basis.

Services used to handle enquiries

When the online form is enabled, Web3Forms processes the form submission to deliver it to the business. hCaptcha is used to help prevent automated abuse when the online form is active. Their services may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser information. WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and Google operate their own services and privacy policies when you follow a link or contact the business there.

This site uses Pexels-hosted stock image placeholders. Loading them shares connection information with the image host. Fonts and the form confirmation library are hosted with the website. The website hosting provider also processes technical request information to serve and protect the site.

Storage, sharing and retention

Information is shared with service providers only as needed to handle enquiries, operate the website and support the agreed work, or where disclosure is required by law. Personal information is retained only for as long as needed for the relevant purpose, including follow-up, service records and applicable record-keeping obligations. Contact Jack for details relating to a particular record.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal information and, where applicable, request deletion, restriction or portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent. Contact Jack using the details above. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office .

Cookies and optional analytics

No advertising or analytics tracking is enabled in this version of the website. Cookie preferences are stored on your device. See the cookie notice or use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice.
