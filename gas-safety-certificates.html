What your inspection covers

The inspection includes gas tightness testing, appliance safety and operation, ventilation and flues, CO alarm checks, pipework condition and the LPG supply equipment. Regulators, meters, hoses and operating pressures are checked as appropriate to your installation.

Clear records, without the paperwork pile

Certificates and invoices are supplied digitally. Jack’s certification system also provides reminders when the next inspection is due, helping owners and park operators keep track of upcoming checks.

If something needs attention

Any defects or safety concerns are explained, together with the remedial work required. The appropriate Gas Safety Certificate is issued on satisfactory completion. Where applicable, heating-system antifreeze or inhibitor protection can also be checked.

How much is an LPG gas safety certificate?

An LPG gas safety inspection and certificate costs £85. A certificate is provided on satisfactory completion. Combine it with the annual service of one appliance for £130, saving £40 compared with booking separately.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes. JTS LPG Services is Gas Safe registered, number 958399. You can check the business and the engineer’s current LPG categories on the Gas Safe Register.

Do you cover the whole of Anglesey?

Yes, including Holyhead, Valley, Trearddur Bay, Rhosneigr, Llangefni, Amlwch, Benllech, Menai Bridge and Beaumaris. Selected work in Bangor and wider North Wales is available by arrangement. Any additional travel costs are agreed beforehand.
