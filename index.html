01 Do you work on static caravans and holiday homes?

Yes. Static caravans, holiday homes and lodges are one of the main areas JTS LPG Services specialises in. Jack also works on domestic LPG properties and provides LPG services for individual owners, landlords, caravan parks and holiday parks.

02 Are you a Gas Safe registered LPG engineer?

Yes. JTS LPG Services is Gas Safe registered for LPG work, registration number 958399. The business and current LPG competencies can be checked through the official Gas Safe Register.

03 How often should static caravan LPG appliances be serviced?

LPG gas appliances should generally be serviced annually, while also following the appliance manufacturer's specific servicing requirements. Regular servicing can identify wear, deterioration or developing faults before they become breakdowns and helps keep boilers, water heaters, gas fires and cookers operating safely and reliably.

Rented holiday accommodation may also have specific gas safety and certification requirements.

04 Which parts of Anglesey do you cover?

JTS LPG Services covers the whole of Anglesey. Regular coverage includes Holyhead, Valley, Trearddur Bay, Rhosneigr, Llangefni, Amlwch, Benllech, Menai Bridge, Beaumaris and surrounding areas.

Selected LPG work is also available in Bangor and across wider North Wales depending on the type and size of the job. Any additional travel costs are agreed beforehand.

05 Do you work directly with caravan and holiday parks?

Yes. JTS LPG Services works with individual owners, caravan parks, holiday parks and site operators across Anglesey and North Wales.

Work can include multiple LPG gas safety inspections, annual boiler and appliance servicing, fault finding, repairs, regulator and hose replacement, gas-supply maintenance and ongoing seasonal LPG servicing across multiple caravans or lodges.

06 Do you repair and replace LPG caravan boilers?

Yes. Jack provides LPG boiler servicing, fault finding, repairs and boiler replacement or installation for static caravans, holiday homes, lodges and domestic LPG properties.

Fault finding can cover heating and hot-water problems, ignition faults, flame-failure issues, gas-pressure problems, regulator or supply faults, leaks and combustion or appliance safety issues.

07 Do you provide plumbing for newly sited caravans?

Yes, as part of caravan and leisure connection work. Jack can provide plumbing and waste connections for newly sited static caravans, holiday homes and lodges, including sinks, showers, basins and appliance waste pipework into the site's drainage system.

This can be combined with the LPG connection, appliance commissioning and Gas Safety Certificate as a complete caravan connection package. JTS LPG Services does not currently offer general domestic plumbing or small plumbing repairs as a standalone service.

08 Are you qualified to work on LPG gas meters?

Yes. Jack holds the MET1 qualification, allowing appropriate gas meter work including installation, exchange and associated safety checks as part of LPG installations. Work is carried out in accordance with current Gas Safe requirements and relevant regulations.

09 What information should I send when asking for a quote?

Send your name, contact number, location, property type and a brief description of the LPG work required. If known, include the make and model of the appliance. Photos can also be useful for breakdowns, repairs and quotations.

For caravan parks, the site name and caravan or plot number are also helpful.
