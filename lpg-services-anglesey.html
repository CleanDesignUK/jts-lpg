LPG Gas Engineer FAQs

LPG SERVICE, REPAIR & INSTALLATION QUESTIONS.

Straight answers about LPG gas safety, boiler servicing, caravan boiler repairs, installations and caravan gas work.

01 How much is an LPG gas safety certificate?

An LPG Gas Safety Inspection and Certificate is £85.

The inspection can include gas-tightness testing, appliance operation and safety checks, ventilation, flues, carbon monoxide alarms, pipework, regulators, meters, hoses and operating pressures.

02 How much does an LPG boiler or appliance service cost?

An annual LPG appliance service is £85.

Servicing can include inspection and cleaning, combustion analysis, gas-pressure checks, performance testing, flue and ventilation checks and testing of appliance safety devices.

03 Can I book my LPG gas safety check and annual service together?

Yes. The combined Gas Safety and Annual Service Package is £130.

It includes the LPG gas safety inspection, certificate and annual service of one appliance during the same visit, saving £40 compared with booking both £85 services separately.

04 Can you repair LPG boilers in static caravans and holiday homes?

Yes. JTS LPG Services provides LPG boiler fault finding and repairs for static caravans, holiday homes, lodges and other LPG properties.

Problems can include no heating, no hot water, ignition faults, flame failure, gas-pressure problems and regulator or supply faults.

05 Do you replace and install LPG boilers?

Yes. LPG boiler replacement and installation is available for static caravans, holiday homes, lodges and appropriate domestic LPG properties.

Replacement work is quoted individually based on the existing installation and the work required.

06 Can you connect and commission a newly sited static caravan?

Yes. New caravan connection work can include LPG supply connection, installation testing, appliance commissioning, ventilation checks, CO checks and certification.

Associated waste plumbing can also be included as part of a full caravan connection package.

07 Which areas do you cover for LPG gas work?

JTS LPG Services covers Anglesey, including Holyhead, Valley, Trearddur Bay, Rhosneigr, Llangefni, Amlwch, Benllech, Menai Bridge and Beaumaris.

Selected work can also be arranged in Bangor and elsewhere in North Wales depending on the job.
